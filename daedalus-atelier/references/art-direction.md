# Art direction — the visual thesis

The second discipline. It turns "make it look good" into a written position that can reject a draft without the art director in the room.

---

## 1. Write the thesis first

One paragraph, four parts: the subject's world, the feeling, the reference points, and the one risk.

**BAD:** "Modern, clean, minimal, with a friendly feel."
Every brief says this; it rejects nothing.

**BETTER:** "A clinic front desk at 7 a.m.: calm, legible at arm's length, nothing that blinks. References: hospital wayfinding signage and Swiss railway timetables — big numerals, strict grid. The risk: appointment times set larger than patient names, because time is what the receptionist scans for."

**Test:** hand the thesis and two drafts to someone who has not seen the brief. If they cannot say which draft is off-thesis, rewrite the thesis.

---

## 2. Derive the choices from the thesis

Each choice carries a one-line reason that points back at the thesis. A choice with no reason is a preference.

| Choice | Decide | Reason must name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | 1 primary, 1 accent, neutrals, status colours | what in the subject's world the colours come from |
| Type | display + body pairing, scale ratio, weights | what the type must do (scan, read long, shout) |
| Grid & density | columns, gutter, information density | the reading distance and the task speed |
| Imagery | photo / illustration / none, and its treatment | what the audience trusts |
| Motion | where, how long, what easing | what the motion explains — never decoration alone |

Fill the semantic token tier from these choices.

---

## 3. Three "never" rules

Negative rules transfer taste faster than positive ones. Write exactly three.

- Never use a gradient behind text.
- Never more than two type weights on one screen.
- Never an animation longer than 200 ms on a repeated action.

---

## 4. Moodboard, if one helps

A moodboard is evidence for the thesis, not a replacement for it. 6–12 references, each captioned with *what to take from it* ("the numeral size", not "nice").

**When not to make one:** the brand already exists, or the job is one screen. A moodboard over an established system invites re-litigating decisions already made.

---

## 5. Check before handing on

- [ ] Thesis names subject, feeling, references, one risk
- [ ] Every palette and type choice has a reason pointing at the thesis
- [ ] Contrast of text colours against their backgrounds checked, with the ratio written down (WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI components)
- [ ] Three "never" rules written
- [ ] The human with final taste has seen the thesis — ask once, with your default stated

## Handoff to creativity

Pass forward: the thesis, the filled semantic tokens, and the never-rules. They become the constraints concepts are scored against.
